## Current paid-tier summary

| Plan | Price | Created Orders / cycle | Total Products |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Starter | 29,000 Ks/month | 60 | 100 |
| Business | 79,000 Ks/month | 200 | 500 |

A successfully created valid order consumes one entitlement and is not refunded because of later seller rejection, cancellation, buyer no-show, RTO, or refund.

Core selling features are not plan-locked merely to force an upgrade. Business is differentiated by scale, speed, staff/workflow, bulk operations, automation/integrations, and other operational leverage.

Business-only paid add-ons are optional and separate from the 79,000 Ks subscription.
